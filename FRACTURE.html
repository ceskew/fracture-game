Core Pillars

  • HP = Currency: Every point in the agreed pool builds your roster. Weight classes (Swarm, Skirmisher, Champion, Titan) scale stats & passives automatically.
  • Shared Action Economy: Both sides get 4 AP per turn—Titans cleave and double-strike; Swarms flank with Pack Tactics.
  • Wound Dials: Each unit's outer ring shifts across Fresh, Bloodied, and Critical tiers as it takes damage, dynamically altering its stats.
  • Blood-Cost Cards: Spend 0 AP—pay directly from a unit's HP to teleport, shield, surge, or siphon.

Turn Flow (4 AP Shared):
Move = 1 AP (moves up to MOV tiles around obstacles, not 1 AP/tile; 1 move/unit/turn; cannot move after attacking) · Attack = 2 AP (1 attack/unit/turn; Titans get 2) · Role Special = 2 AP (Execute & Overcharge consume your attack; Fortify & Transfuse do not) · Blood Card = 0 AP (paid in unit HP).
Shield Decay: Shields halve (50% decay) at the start of your next turn.
Turn 1 Free Deploy: Click gold back-row tiles to reposition for 0 AP before your first move/attack. Press Z to undo a move, Esc to cancel, Space to end turn.
2d6 RNG Combat Rolls & Piercing:
• 2–3 GLANCE: 65% damage · 4–9 HIT: 100% · 10–11 HEAVY: 125% · 12 CRIT!: 150% & ignores 100% Armor.
• Piercing: Arcanist attacks & Overcharge pierce 50% of Armor. All piercing & Crit damage is still absorbed by Shield first.
Terrain Guide:
• Tree (Green): Impassable (go around), blocks LOS.
• Water (Blue): Impassable to walk, ranged can shoot across.
• Brush (Cover): Walkable, grants +2 ARM.
• Pillar (Dark): Impassable, blocks LOS.
• Conduit (Brass): Walkable, +2 ATK & +1 RNG.
• Rift (Violet): -3 HP at end of turn (+5 if pushed in).
Fighter Roles:
• ◈ Vanguard (Tank): Frontline bruiser with high Armor; FORTIFY adds +6 Shield (max 10; halves next turn) and Last Stand spikes ATK/ARM at low HP.
• ▲ Striker (Assassin): Fast melee flanker (4 MOV, high ATK); EXECUTE (uses attack) deals +5 bonus damage to wounded foes.
• ✦ Arcanist (Ranged): Long-range caster (3 RNG) that pierces 50% Armor; OVERCHARGE (uses attack) burns 4 HP for a +3 DMG Range-4 blast.
• ✚ Catalyst (Support): Tactical healer/mid-range fighter; TRANSFUSE (does not use attack) transfers 8 HP to an ally within 3 tiles.
Weight Classes & Wound Dials:
• Swarm (<25 HP): +1 MOV, Pack Tactics (+2 ATK/adj ally).
• Skirmisher (25–49): Flanker (+2 ATK vs flanked foe).
• Champion (50–79): +1 ARM, Momentum (+1 AP on kill).
• Titan (80+): +2 ARM, Cleave (50% splash), 2 attacks/turn.
• Dial Tiers: Green (>66%) → Amber (34–66%) → Red (≤33%).
